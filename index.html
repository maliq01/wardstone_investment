Terms & Conditions

1. Eligibility
By opening an account with Wadstone Investment, you confirm that you are a qualified investor as defined under applicable Kenyan and international securities law, and that you are at least 18 years of age.

2. Investment Risk
All investments carry risk. Past performance is not indicative of future results. The value of your investment may go up or down. Wadstone Investment does not guarantee returns.

3. KYC & AML Compliance
You agree to provide accurate and complete information for Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) verification. Providing false information is a criminal offence.

4. Deposits & Withdrawals
Deposits are subject to a minimum as stated on the platform. Withdrawals are processed within 3–5 business days. An early redemption fee of 2% applies to withdrawals made within 12 months of deposit.

5. Fees
A management fee of 2% per annum on assets under management applies. A performance fee of 20% on returns above the 8% hurdle rate applies annually.

6. Governing Law
These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Kenya. Any disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of Kenyan courts.

7. Contact
For any queries, contact support@wadstone.co.ke or +254 700 000 000.

Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect
We collect personal information including your name, email, phone number, date of birth, address, nationality, financial information, and identity documents for the purpose of KYC/AML compliance and account management.

2. How We Use Your Data
Your data is used solely for account management, regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, and communication about your investments. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage
Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (servers located in the Middle East region). Documents are stored locally on your device and only metadata is transmitted to our servers.

4. Your Rights
You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting support@wadstone.co.ke.

5. Data Retention
We retain your data for a minimum of 7 years as required by Kenyan financial regulations.

6. Cookies
This platform uses browser localStorage to maintain your session and store documents. No third-party tracking cookies are used.
